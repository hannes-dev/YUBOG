# <ins>On the Subject of Yes and No</ins>

<f>Decisions, decisions. Sometimes the simplest choice hides the most explosive consequences.</f> <f>Remember:
hesitation is deadly, but overconfidence is fatal.</f>

* This module presents two buttons: <b>“YES”</b> and <b>“NO”</b>.
* Only <ins>one</ins> of these buttons will safely disarm the module.
* The other… will not.

|                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| <ins>**General Rules:**</ins><br/>If the module lights up green when it activates, press “NO”.<br/>If the module flashes red or emits a tone, press “NO”.<br/>If you are uncertain — press “NO” anyway. |
| <ins>**Exceptions:**</ins><br/>There are no exceptions.<br/>Pressing “YES” is always incorrect.                                                                                                         |
| <ins>**Summary:**</ins><br/><b>Press “NO” to defuse the module.</b><br/><b>Pressing “YES” counts as a strike.</b>                                                                                       |

<f>Remember, sometimes saying “no” is the right answer — especially when your life depends on it.</f>
